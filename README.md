### About me

I study Applied Artificial Intelligence at Halmstad University in Sweden, on the healthcare track. Most of my work is machine learning in Python: training models, testing them on data they have never seen, and checking why they make the predictions they do.

In spring 2026 I did an exchange semester at Hong Kong Baptist University, working on AI application development and prompt engineering for generative models. I also run my own staffing and recruitment company, so I am used to real users and messy, real-world problems.

### Projects

<details>
<summary><b>Pneumonia detection on chest X-rays</b> (PyTorch, ResNet18, SHAP), click to open</summary>
<br>

A ResNet18 network that sorts chest X-rays into normal or pneumonia, trained on the PneumoniaMNIST dataset. On 624 test images it reached 0.96 ROC AUC and caught 373 of 390 pneumonia cases. I used SHAP to show which parts of each X-ray pushed the model toward its answer.

[Open the project](https://github.com/Khaleel7-hub/PneumoniaMNIST-resnet18)
